Contact Vid.Best

If a review needs a correction, a link is broken, or you have a rights or privacy concern, you can contact Vid.Best using the email address below.

Email contact

info@vid.best

Include a clear subject such as “Review correction”, “Copyright or attribution”, “Privacy request”, or “Technical problem”. For page-specific reports, include the full vid.best/watch/… link and a brief description of the issue. You do not need to provide account passwords, login tokens or other sensitive credentials.

Creators and rights holders

If you believe a featured video, screenshot, thumbnail or review misrepresents your work, tell us the relevant URL, your concern and the correction or removal you are requesting. We may need enough information to understand the work and your connection to it.

Readers and community

For factual corrections, please identify the specific claim and, if available, provide a trustworthy source. You may also report a player that fails to load or an outdated original link. For information about comments, accounts and technical data, please see the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Messages are handled as resources permit; email delivery and response times are not guaranteed.
