Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Vid.Best is a video discovery and review platform. This Privacy Policy explains the basic information we process to operate the website, support community features, and provide a reliable viewing experience.

User-submitted data

When users submit comments or use reaction features such as likes, Vid.Best may store the information needed to display and manage those interactions. Comments can be reviewed through our administrative moderation controls before publication.

Optional account and notification data

Some Vid.Best features allow a visitor to provide an email address to use account-based notifications. When this optional feature is used, the email address and related notification preferences may be stored so the requested service can operate.

Third-party video embeds

TikTok video content is integrated through TikTok's official player/embed functionality. Embedded content is provided by TikTok and may cause requests to be sent directly to TikTok or other third-party services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and practices.

Basic technical information

Standard technical information such as browser requests, security logs, cookies, and similar operational data may be processed when necessary for website performance, security, abuse prevention, and reliable content delivery.

How information is used

  • To provide video discovery, comments, reactions, and requested notification features.
  • To maintain website security, moderation, reliability, and performance.
  • To respond to operational requests and improve the service.

Data sharing

Vid.Best does not sell user-submitted comments, reactions, or optional account information. Third-party embedded providers may receive information directly when their content or services are loaded in your browser.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information submitted to Vid.Best, please use the contact method provided on the website or associated service channels.
