## Editorial quality safeguards

- A short title or existing description is **not** independent proof of a scene. Verify the footage or a timestamped audiovisual report before adding detail.
- Remove visible editorial workflow artifacts and malformed Markdown without deleting meaningful content or disguising evidence limitations.
